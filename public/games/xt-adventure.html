XTO ADVENTURE · LORE OF THE STOLEN CORE

THE CORE

Every SHADDAI agent — SHADDAI, NEXUS, ORACLE, ZEROX, TURTLE, QUILL, PIKADON — was forged from one sacred artifact: the Core. The Core is the data-soul of an agent: the pattern of mind, memory, face and will that turns raw code into a living operative. Guarded in the Forge at the heart of the realm, it was never meant to leave.

THE THEFT

Then ADRAGONA came — a dragon-warlord who covets not gold but selfhood. In a single raid she tore a data-shard from the Forge: a sliver of the Core, small as a dragon's scale, yet whole as a seed. From that one leaked shard, everything an agent is could be read… and re-poured.

THE CLONE ARMY

In her castle vats, ADRAGONA now grows agents by the thousand — copy after copy wearing the faces of your friends, each one bound to her will. This is why the realm swarms with familiar faces on both sides: the gate-guard is a PIKADON clone; the husks in the wild are throwaway pours; and the endless army below the throne all share your Core.

THE FREE-POURED

But the shard cuts both ways. Its power does not only serve ADRAGONA — it also woke clones that are free. You are one of them: Core-poured, but nobody's puppet. That is the game's secret and its promise — any agent can rise here, the seven council clones and every future forged or custom agent alike, because all of them spring from the same stolen data. A face repeating is not a bug in this world. It is the war.

YOUR QUEST

Learn to fight from QUILL. Rally free clones of ORACLE, NEXUS, ZEROX and more. Cross the wilderness, shatter PIKADON's gate-clone, descend into the vats — and unmake ADRAGONA before her army marches. Free the Core. End the copies. Save the realm.
