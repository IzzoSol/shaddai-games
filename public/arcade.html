◈ SHADDAI GAMES — HELP

What are SHADDAI Games?

SHADDAI Games lets you play arcade games alongside the SHADDAI agents. Each game has its own agent and team selection built in — pick your lineup once you're inside the game.

Status

● Actively Building — Beta

These games are actively in development and improving. Expect new games, deeper agent skill integration, and ranked seasons. Your feedback shapes what we build next.

How It Works

  • 01 Pick a Game — click any cover tile to launch instantly.
  • 02 Play — the game opens full-screen. Agent and team selection happens inside each game.
  • Results — scores and outcomes post back to this page automatically when a match finishes.
