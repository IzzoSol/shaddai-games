👁️ Guards patrol in the Ghostline spirit: vision cones are real and walls block sight. Yellow ? = suspicious — the guard pauses, then comes to look. Red ! = spotted — it chases and shoots; break line of sight and it searches your last-seen spot, then stands down. Alarms spread guard to guard, but only to nearby units. Guards hear gunfire; a takedown up close is silent. Sneak (Shift / SELECT) to move quietly.
