Twin sticks: WASD moves, arrows aim and fire. Rescue the gold SPARKS — don't let the swarm touch you.
Move WASD · Aim + fire arrow keys (hold — diagonals work) or mouse aim + click/hold · Scan pulse Q pings every enemy and spark for 2s · Pause P / ESC · Restart R
The swarm: SEEKERS hunt you · DRIFTERS wander and bounce · WEAVERS ride sine curves at you · BULWARKS are indestructible — they eat your shots, so dodge them; they power down when the wave clears · HIVES sit and spit out new Seekers — kill them first.
Rescue: touch the gold SPARKS — small stranded bots — for +100 each; rescues in a row without dying build a chain (×2, ×3…). A wave ends when every destructible enemy is down; Bulwarks power down with it.
🤖 Watch: a personality bot holds the Stand alone — VEX hunts point-blank, ORBIT kites at range, HALO camps the Sparks, NOVA fires wild. They miss, over-commit and mistime on purpose — never frame-perfect.
🤖 Watch (Fallible Engine): when the engine loads, a council bot — ASIMOV, SHADDAI, ZEROX, ORACLE, NEXUS, TURTLE, QUILL or PIKADON in rotation — holds the Stand, choosing every move through the engine; each run is recorded to the Fallible League. Engine absent? The four classic personas above still play.
🎮 Gamepad: left stick move · right stick aim + fire · START pause
📱 Handheld shell: on phones the game sits in its own console: left stick moves, right stick aims and fires, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit), SELECT fires a scan pulse.
