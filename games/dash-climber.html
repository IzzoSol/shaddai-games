KIP the courier-bot climbs the Shatterpeak. One dash per leap — spend it well.
Controls: ← → / A D run, Z / K / SPACE jump, X / J / SHIFT dash (8-way — hold a direction), ↑ ↓ aim the dash up / down. Slide down walls and press jump to wall-jump. Spikes end the run back at the room start; springs bounce; dash crystals refill your dash; grab the sparks. V toggles climber-cam, P pauses, M mutes, R restarts the room.
📱 Handheld shell: on phones the game sits in its own console: D-pad to run and aim the dash, A jumps, B dashes, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit), SELECT toggles climber-cam.
Clean-room note: dash-climb mechanics follow the documented behaviour of the classic PICO-8 dash platformer (spec lane — read as text only, rewritten here in our own code). The mountain, the rooms, KIP and every line of art are original.
