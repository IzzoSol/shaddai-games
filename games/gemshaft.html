Dig the shaft. Mind the boulders.
How to play: You are PIKE, a shaft-runner bot. Dig dirt (+10), collect SPARK-GEMS (+50) until the quota is met — then the EXIT PORTAL opens and you run for it before the timer seals the shaft. Boulders and gems obey gravity: they fall when unsupported and roll off round things. A falling rock on your head ends a life. Push boulders sideways to drop them on pests: a SPARKFLY pops, a GEMMOTH bursts into gems. GLOAM grows through dirt — box it in and it turns to gems.
Controls: arrows / WASD dig + steer · SHIFT / SPACE dash-step · V lantern cam · P pause · M mute · R restart.
📱 Handheld shell: on phones the game sits in its own console: D-pad steers PIKE, A dash-steps, B toggles lantern cam, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit), SELECT toggles lantern cam.
WATCH is a council bot miner on the Fallible Engine — ZEROX rushes gems and ignores falling rock, TURTLE digs safe and slow, NEXUS balances the two. They misjudge on purpose — never frame-perfect. If the engine fails to load, the old ROOK / SPARK / DRIFT stand-ins dig instead.
