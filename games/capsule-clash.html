Drop capsules, match 4+ of a colour, and clear every glitch-bot out of the jar.
Move ← / → or A / D · Rotate ↑ / W / X or tap A · Soft drop ↓ / S · Slam SPACE / Z or tap B — drops the capsule instantly · Pause P / ESC · Restart R · Hint H shows the ghost of a good placement.
The jar: capsules fall two halves at a time. Line up 4+ of one colour in a row or column and the whole run clears — including any glitch-bots caught in it: BLIP (cyan), ZORP (magenta), GLEEK (gold). Clears chain when the pieces above fall into new matches — chains multiply the score. Clear every glitch-bot to take the level; the next jar seeds more bots and falls faster. Stack to the top and the jar tops out.
Versus: clearing bots and chaining sends grey slag raining into your rival's jar — slag only shatters when a clear goes off next to it. First to clear their jar, or last jar standing, wins. WATCH is two personality bots duelling — VEX fast and aggressive, ORBIT patient and precise, HALO hunts chains, NOVA plays erratic. They misdrop and mistime on purpose — never frame-perfect.
📱 Handheld shell: on phones the game sits in its own console: D-pad moves and soft-drops, ▲ rotates, A rotates, B slams, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit), SELECT toggles the hint.
⛶ Fullscreen: F or the Fullscreen button — the stage (on phones, the whole handheld shell) fills the screen; Esc exits fullscreen.
