Found Embervale: zone homes, shops and works, keep the lights on, and grow it to 5,000 citizens.
How to build: pick a tool below the map (or press B to cycle), move the cursor with arrows / WASD, place with Enter / Space / A or click a tile. Roads carry power and people — zones only grow beside a road. Coal plants ($400, 700⚡) and wind turbines ($150, 150⚡) power everything they can reach through roads and zones; unpowered zones stall and dim. Homes want parks nearby and hate industry next door. Taxes pay every month; upkeep eats at them. Fires start in dry months — bulldoze the burning tile before it spreads. Reach 5,000 citizens for EMBERVALE PRIME. Fall below -$500 and the vale goes bankrupt.
📱 Handheld shell: on phones the game sits in its own console: D-pad moves the cursor, A places the tool, B cycles tools, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit), SELECT toggles street cam.
