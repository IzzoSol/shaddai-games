WORDCLASH — crossword tile battle on our own 15×15 board with our own bonus map and our own tile distribution (100 tiles). The word list is the public-domain ENABLE list (168,548 words, 2–15 letters). First word must cover the ★ centre. Every play forms a main word plus any cross words — and nothing is checked until the challenge: a play stands unless the opponent calls it. Bluff a word that is not in the list and you score — unless you are caught: a caught bluff loses the play and costs 10. A wrong challenge costs the challenger 10.
Controls: click a rack tile, then click a board square to place it · click a placed tile to take it back · 1–7 pick a rack tile · arrows / WASD move the cursor, ENTER / SPACE place · C commit · X clear draft · V rack-level camera · P pause (save / load in the menu) · R restart · M mute.
⛶ Fullscreen (F): the shell fills the screen — Esc exits first, P still pauses.
BOTS DO WORDPLAY BATTLES: WATCH is bot-vs-bot on the Fallible Engine — QUILL knows the weird words and almost never bluffs, ZEROX gambles on bluffs and wild challenges, TURTLE plays safe short words. Every decision is recorded as a trace. ROOM — SOON the in-world room view ships only when it genuinely reads as a board sitting on your real table; until a real device verifies it, flat + rack view are the verified modes and AR is SIM.
📱 Handheld shell: D-pad moves the cursor, A places the selected rack tile, B cycles rack tiles, SELECT commits, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit). A saved game is a state string in localStorage.
Clean-room: crossword tile-play mechanics only — no borrowed name, board, tile distribution or art. Word list: ENABLE (public domain).
