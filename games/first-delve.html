FIRST DELVE — Delver-bot SERIN crosses the castle maze to fetch the Glow Chalice and carry it home to the Gold Gate. Three dragons patrol the halls — VERDANT guards the chalice, EMBERMAW hunts anything that moves, GILDED paces the depths — and the thieving bat SNATCHER will steal whatever you carry and drop it somewhere strange. Touch an item to pick it up (one at a time); SPACE / E drops it. The Sword of Dawn slays a dragon on touch — slain dragons crawl home and return after a while. The Ember Key opens the Ember Gate, the Gold Key opens the Gold Gate shortcut home, the Bridge spans the moat, and the Magnet pulls loose metal to you. Eaten without a sword? You lose a life and drop what you carried. Three lives. Somewhere in the castle there is a hidden room — the genre's founding trick, honoured with one of our own.
Controls: Arrows / WASD move · SPACE / E take / drop · V delver-cam · P pause (save / load in the menu) · M mute · R restart. In 2P, player 2 (ORIN, cyan) moves with ← → ↑ ↓ and takes/drops with ENTER.
WATCH is a council bot delver on the Fallible Engine — it plans the route (sword, key, chalice, home), flees dragons when unarmed, and slays them when armed. It misjudges on purpose — never frame-perfect. If the engine fails to load, the legacy SERIN stand-in delves instead.
📱 Handheld shell: D-pad moves SERIN, TAKE picks up / drops, B toggles delver-cam, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit).
