HOW TO PLAY

TYPES — EMBER burns LEAF + BLOOM · LEAF drinks TIDE · TIDE douses EMBER · SPARK shocks TIDE + GALE · STONE crushes EMBER + SPARK · SHADE haunts BLOOM + GALE · BLOOM roots STONE + TIDE · GALE scatters EMBER + LEAF. Same-type moves hit 1.5× (STAB).
CATCHING — weaken a wild Spark, then throw a SNARE ORB (◉). Low HP + a status = a far better catch. Caught Sparks join your party (max 6) or the box.
GROWTH — win battles for XP. Creatures evolve at level 16 (starters + wild lines) — evolutions visibly grow the design. Story state, party and box save to this browser automatically.
CONTROLS — Explore: WASD walk, drag to look, E talk, C classic top-down view. Battle: click a move, ◉ throw an orb, Run to flee wild battles. Mobile: D-pad + A interact / B back, START for save & load.
