STARCLASH — a gravity-well ship melee, built clean-room from the documented mechanics of the ship-duel formula (SPEC lane: The Ur-Quan Masters is a complete GPL port whose Super Melee mode is a complete standalone duel arena; its code was read as a design donor only and its CC BY-NC-SA content is not reusable — every ship, pilot, name and line of text here is original). Two ships duel around the ringed giant CALDRA: its gravity bends every flight path, its surface is a death sentence, and the arena wraps edge to edge. Shots cost energy, energy regrows, and after a minute of stalemate the star FLARES and burns both crews.

WATCH is two council bots duelling on the Fallible Engine — each picks its next move from scored options by personality, so it overshoots the well, wastes its special, and tilts after a bad round. Never frame-perfect. If the engine fails to load, ORBIT stand-ins fly instead.
