HOW TO PLAY — Click a green unit to select it. Green hexes are its move range; red hexes are attack targets. Move, then strike — or press A on the unit again to hold position. SENTRY and DART capture cities, factories and the enemy HQ — step on one to start, then hold it for two turns while the banner changes; a beeline alone cannot steal an HQ. Factories build new units for credits — click your factory, then a unit button (or 1–5). Every city and factory you hold pays +50 credits at the start of your turn. Win by taking the enemy HQ or wiping their army.
UNITS — DART scout · move 5, hits 4, fast and fragile, captures. SENTRY trooper · move 3, hits 5, captures. PIKE lancer · move 4, hits 8, breaks troopers and guns. LONGSHOT artillery · move 2, hits 10 at range 2–3, cannot hit adjacent. KESTREL skyhawk · move 6, hits 6, flies over rivers and forests.
CONTROLS — Mouse click to select / move / attack · Arrows / WASD move cursor · Enter / Space select · E end turn · V commander-cam · P pause · M mute · R restart.
