HOW IT WORKS, HONESTLY

iPhone / iPad (Safari): AR Quick Look opens the cabinet (USDZ) and anchors it in your room at real size — point at the floor, move to place. Place-and-view only: the screen shows baked cover art. A live, playable game feed inside Quick Look is not possible, so live play stays in the flat game.
Android (Chrome): WebXR where the device supports it, Scene Viewer otherwise — same cabinet (GLB), placed in your room at real size.
Desktop / anything else: no fake AR. You get the 3D preview above and a ROOM — SOON marker instead of a view we cannot honestly offer.
