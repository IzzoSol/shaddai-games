Cinderfall is burning. The deep is calling.
EMBERDEEP — an isometric-ARPG dungeon slice, built clean-room from the documented mechanics of the Diablo formula (SPEC lane: the complete DevilutionX reconstruction was read as design reference only — it carries a non-commercial caveat, so no code or asset was reused; every room, name, creature and line of text here is original). KAEL, the emberblade, leaves Cinderfall and descends a seeded dungeon — one seed, one dungeon — through ember halls, a crypt, a treasure vault and a shrine room, to face MALACHAR, the Ashen King. Cut down mites, skulls and hounds, gather gold, drink potions, take the Sunforged Blade from the chest, and kneel at the Shrine of Embers for its blessing.
Arrows / WASD move · J / Z / SPACE sword · K / X potion · E use (shrine / chest / merchant) · SHIFT dash · V hero-cam · P pause · R restart · M mute. BRAZ the smith sells potions (50g) and the Sunforged Edge (+8 damage, 200g) — stand by his stall and press E.
WATCH is one council bot delving through the Fallible Engine — it picks its next move from scored options by personality, so it chases the wrong room, wastes potions, and tilts after a bad fight. Never frame-perfect. If the engine fails to load, the ORBIT stand-in delves instead.
📱 Handheld shell: on phones the game sits in its own console: D-pad moves, A sword, B potion, » dash, USE works shrines, chests and the merchant, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit).
