The coast waits.

JUNO takes the VERDIGRIS coupe down the Emerald Coast. Beat the clock, beat the traffic.
Controls: ← → / A D steer, ↑ / W accelerate, ↓ / S brake, X nitro (3 charges), V cockpit cam, P pause, M mute, R restart stage.
📱 Handheld shell: on phones the game sits in its own console: D-pad steers, A accelerates, B brakes, N fires nitro, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit), SELECT toggles cockpit cam.
Rules: five stages down the Emerald Coast — Emerald Coast → Sunset Dunes → Neon City → Pine Pass → Ember Finish. Each stage ends at a checkpoint gantry: cross it before the clock hits zero and the timer tops up +30s with +1000 score. Miss the clock and the run is over. Traffic is live — clip a car and you spin down; thread past one close for a NEAR MISS +50. Off the road the coupe bogs down. Finish stage 5 to win the Grand Tour.
Clean-room note: pseudo-3D road rendering follows the classic documented segment-projection technique (SPEC lane — read as a technique only, rewritten here in our own code; no code, art, names or tracks copied). The coast, JUNO, the VERDIGRIS coupe and every line of art are original.
