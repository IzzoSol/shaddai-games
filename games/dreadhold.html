DREADHOLD — a one-level raycast maze FPS. MARA VOSS, warden-bot, walks the hold with a pulse rifle: find the CORE KEY in the north-west vault, open the CORE GATE, and step through the EXIT PORTAL in the south-east vault. SCRAP HUSKS rush you, SPORE SPITTERS lob globs from range, and WARDEN PRIME — 200 HP of armoured spite — guards the gate.
W/S move · A/D strafe · ←/→ turn (or drag the view) · SHIFT run · SPACE / click fire · V automap · P / ESC pause · M mute · R restart.
WATCH is a council bot clearing the hold on the Fallible Engine — it pathfinds to the key, the gate and the portal, aims at what it can see, and blunders on purpose. Never frame-perfect. DUEL is two alternate runs, one per player — high score takes the hold.
📱 Handheld shell: D-pad moves and turns (▲▼ walk, ◀▶ turn), A fires, B toggles the automap, SELECT switches the D-pad to strafe, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit).
