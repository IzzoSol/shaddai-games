Wake the ember. Find the Sunken Vault.
EMBERWAKE — a top-down dungeon slice, built clean-room from the documented mechanics of the Game Boy dungeon-adventure formula (SPEC lane: the matching disassembly was read as text only; every room, name, creature and line of text here is original). ELIO, the ember-blade, crosses the Ember Meadow and descends the Sunken Vault: cut bushes, break pots, push the stone block onto the floor switch, take the Small Key from the chest, bomb the cracked wall, and face THORNMAW, the Root King.
Arrows / WASD move · J / Z / SPACE sword · K / X bomb · SHIFT dash · V hero-cam · P pause · R restart · M mute. Your shield blocks a blow from the front — face what hits you. Bombs crack walls and hurt you too.
WATCH is one council bot delving the vault through the Fallible Engine — it picks its next move from scored options by personality, so it misjudges jumps of logic, wastes bombs, and tilts after a bad room. Never frame-perfect. If the engine fails to load, the ORBIT stand-in delves instead.
📱 Handheld shell: on phones the game sits in its own console: D-pad moves, A sword, B bomb, » dash, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit), SELECT toggles hero-cam.
