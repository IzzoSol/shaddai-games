IRONGRID — Warden Juno Vail holds the last Forge Core on the scrapyard grid. Enemy armour rolls in from the north in waves: SCOUT darts (fast, thin), LANCER snipers (long fast shells), BRUISER heavies (three plates of armour, twin barrels) and SWARMER skirmishers (erratic, everywhere). Blast through brick to open lanes — steel holds, water stops your tracks but not your shells, trees hide you. Kill a flashing raider to drop a power-up: STAR upgrades your cannon, SHIELD hardens your hull, LIFE adds a warden, BOMB clears the field. The Forge Core is armoured — the first shell cracks it, the second ends it, yours or theirs.
WATCH is a council bot warden on the Fallible Engine — each stage a different agent drives the tank, choosing its moves by personality, and learning from every stage. They misjudge on purpose — never frame-perfect. If the engine fails to load, the old JUNO / ORBIT / GAMBLER stand-ins defend instead.
Controls: WASD drive · SPACE / click fire · V turret-eye view · P pause · 2P: P2 drives with arrows and fires with ENTER.
📱 Handheld shell: on phones the game sits in its own console: D-pad drives, A fires, B toggles turret-eye view, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit), SELECT toggles turret-eye view.
Clean-room: grid tank-defence formula (documented 26×26 arena, four enemy classes with documented speed/armour/bullet stats, destructible brick / steel / water / trees), re-implemented in our own code. All names, commanders, maps and art are original — no copied code, assets or names.
