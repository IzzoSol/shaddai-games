LIFEPATH — an original life simulator. Guide one character from birth to death, one year per turn. Each year you get 2 actions (study, work out, socialise, hustle, find love, build a career…), then press AGE +1 and take whatever the year brings — events with real choices, all of it written into the Life Log, the story of that life. After 50, health fades; when death comes you get the full summary. Then: a new life.
⚔ DUEL LIVES — two players, one machine, two parallel lives in lockstep. Player 1 lives their year, then Player 2 lives theirs. Watch for crossover moments where the two lives collide — schoolyard meetings, office rivalries, shared hustles. When both lives end, the verdict screen settles it: lifespan, wealth, happiness, family.
👁 WATCH is a council bot living a whole life on the Fallible Engine — ZEROX hustles and gambles, TURTLE studies and saves, QUILL socialises — imperfect on purpose, every decision traced. If the engine fails to load, a stand-in lives the life instead.
📱 Handheld shell: on phones the game sits in its own console: D-pad moves menu focus, A confirms, B ages a year, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit), SELECT flips the log between lives in Duel mode. Keyboard: ←→↑↓ focus · ENTER confirm · A age +1 · P pause.
Clean-room: the life-sim formula, our own code, names, events and art — every event in the pool was written for this game. No other life-sim's text, events or names are used.
