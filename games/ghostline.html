Move WASD / Arrows · Sneak SHIFT · Knock out SPACE · Pause P / ESC · Restart R (click a key above to rebind it — saved on this device)
Guard vision cones are real: walls block sight. Yellow ? = a guard is suspicious — it pauses, then comes and looks where it glimpsed or heard you. Red ! = that guard has you and chases; break line of sight and it searches your last-seen spot, then gives up. Alarms spread guard to guard — only nearby units learn of you. Guards also hear: running and knockouts carry, sneaking is silent. Touch a guard awake and you're caught. The gold door needs the keycard. Structure follows the classic top-down stealth formula documented in the Metal Gear (MSX2) disassembly — every name, room and line of code here is original.
🗺️ Missions: Vault Compound (standard) and Night Yard (hard — 7 guards, faster patrols). Pick one above, then press PLAY — your pick is remembered on this device.
🎮 Gamepad: left stick / D-pad to move · A / X knock out · LB sneak · START pause
💬 Dialogue: radio calls from LOOM, SCRIBE and HQ arrive in a typewriter box with a portrait chip — the speaker's name on the left, queued lines stack up to 3 deep.
🤖 Watch: a council agent infiltrates on its own — the camera follows the run, the name plate (top left) shows the agent's style and mission pips, the compound plate (top right) shows the alert state, and the feed logs the keycard, alerts and extraction.
🎬 Cinematic camera: the mission opens with a slow push-in, knockouts punch the camera in on the fallen guard, and extraction / capture hold a close-up on WRAITH.
⛶ Fullscreen: F or the Fullscreen button — the stage (on phones, the whole handheld shell) fills the screen; Esc exits fullscreen.
📱 Handheld shell: on phones the game sits in its own console: D-pad + A/B below the screen, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit), SELECT locks sneak on/off. Hold A to sneak · tap B to knock out a guard next to you.
