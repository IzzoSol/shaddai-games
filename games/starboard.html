⭐ Briefing: Four travellers race the STARBOARD — a 48-space star-chart loop around the Ember Isles. Roll the die, walk the board, grab coins, and buy stars for 20 coins whenever you land on the glowing star space. After every round the four of you meet in a minigame — STAR SNATCH (stop the needle), COIN CASCADE (memory pairs), DICE DUEL (high roll takes the pot). Ten rounds. Most stars wins; coins break ties.
🎲 Spaces: gold coin +3 · red coin −3 · EVENT (warp, shower, drain, swap) · DUEL (challenge a rival, winner takes 10) · LUCKY (+10, sometimes a free star) · BATTLE (everyone pays 5 into the pot, minigame decides it). Passing START pays +10.
🤖 Watch: four council bots play the whole voyage on the Fallible Engine — they decide star buys, duels and minigame moves through scored options, with traces recorded, and their result is filed at the end. ZEROX gambles, TURTLE saves, ORACLE plays the odds. They blunder on purpose.
🧍 Characters: every traveller is a sprite-stacked robot token — layered slices, rim light, ground shadow — never a flat blob. Press V for the board-level table cam.
📱 Handheld shell: A rolls / confirms, B backs out, SELECT stops the minigame needle, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit).
🧪 Clean-room: party-board formula (dice movement, coin economy, star purchase, round minigames, hot-seat multiplayer — the documented behaviour of the classic party-board games, SPEC lane: read as design reference, never run or copied); our own code, board, travellers, minigames, names and story. VR runs through the shared XR engine and is labelled SIM until a headset verifies it in-world.
