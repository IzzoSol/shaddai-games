JETSTORM — jetpack arena deathmatch in the Ember Yard. Run, jump, burn your jetpack, and put rounds on the other three pilots. First to 10 kills takes the match. Weapon crates drop on the gantries: SPREADER (5-pellet fan), LAUNCHER (explosive shells), RAILGUN (one piercing lance). Medkits and fuel cells spawn too — a dry jetpack is a dead pilot.
WATCH is a 4-bot free-for-all on the Fallible Engine — VEX rushes every fight, NOVA snipes from the high gantry, ORBIT balances the yard, HALO guards the medkit ledge. They misjudge on purpose — never frame-perfect.
WASD / arrows move · SPACE jump · hold SHIFT jetpack · mouse aim · click / J fire · V pilot cam · P pause · M mute · R restart · Duel P2: arrows move, / fire (auto-aim).
📱 Handheld shell: on phones the game sits in its own console: D-pad runs, ▲ jumps, B holds the jetpack, A fires (aim follows your movement / nearest foe), drag on the yard to aim, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit), SELECT toggles the pilot cam.
