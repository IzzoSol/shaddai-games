STACKBREAK — Two courier drones ride opposite passes over the crumbling Cindral canyon: VESK (amber, swept wings) works the west wall, ORIN (cyan, twin pods) works the east. Time your bomb drops — a bomb smashes the brick it lands on and every brick stacked below it in that column (the blast wraps around bedrock, which never breaks). Gold bricks pay +50, bedrock never breaks. First side cleared takes the round; first to 3 rounds takes the match. The wind pushes every bomb sideways — watch the gauge.
Controls: A / D throttle your drone · SPACE drop a bomb. In 2P, player 2 (ORIN) throttles with ← / → and drops with ENTER. V switches to bomber-cam — the camera rides your drone down the canyon. P pauses (save / load in the menu), M mutes, R restarts.
WATCH is a bot duel with distinct styles — ZEROX gambles on any column, TURTLE waits for the tallest stack, NEXUS balances, ORACLE hunts gold. They misjudge on purpose — never frame-perfect.
📱 Handheld shell: D-pad throttles the drone, DROP releases a bomb, B toggles bomber-cam, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit). Tap the canyon to drop too.
