THE ARCADE, IN YOUR ROOM

SHADDAI ARCADE · AR PLACEMENT · SIM ← ARCADE

Put the arcade in your room.

This is augmented reality, not a screen trick: on a capable device the eight cabinets stand in your actual room, at real size — on your floor, against your wall, lit by your light. You place them, you walk around them, you tap a cabinet screen to poke the game it is running.

  1. Aim your device at a clear patch of floor (or a big desk) and tap ENTER AR.
  2. Place — move the glowing reticle to where the arc should stand, then tap (select) to set it down on that real surface. A soft shadow settles under it so it sits on the floor, not above it.
  3. Move it — squeeze a controller (or squeeze gesture) to pick the arc back up, then tap again to set it down somewhere else.
