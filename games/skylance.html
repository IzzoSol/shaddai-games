Flap to fly. The HIGHER lance wins — burst them, grab the core before it hatches back stronger.
Flap W / ↑ / SPACE / tap A — tap in rhythm to climb, don't hold · Steer A / D or ← / → · Dive S / ↓ / B drops you fast onto a ledge or a rival · Rider Cam V zooms to your rider · Pause P / ESC · Restart R
The duel: when two riders collide, the higher lance wins — the loser bursts into a falling core. Touch the core to bank it (+150); leave it and it hatches back stronger. Equal height? Both bounce off. The glowing floor is the Maw — touch it and you're gone, no core. Stall too long and the GALE — a storm-wyrm — sweeps the arena hunting the slow. Fly off a side and you wrap around to the other.
2P Duel: P1 A/D + W flap + S dive · P2 ←/→ + ↑ flap + ↓ dive — first to 3 rounds. A round is won by a Maw kill or by bursting a rival and banking their core.
🤖 Watch: two personality bots duel — VEX dives hard, ORBIT waits and counters, HALO camps the ledges, NOVA flies erratic. They miss, mistime and stall on purpose — never frame-perfect. The camera follows the duel, name plates show each bot's style, and the feed (top right) logs bursts, banks and rounds.
🎮 Gamepad: stick / D-pad steer · A / X flap · B dive · START pause
📱 Handheld shell: on phones the game sits in its own console: D-pad steers, A flaps, B dives, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit), SELECT toggles Rider Cam.
🎬 Intro: a short comic plays before every match — SPACE / click advances, ESC skips the story (START skips it on the handheld shell).
💬 Dialogue: LOOM and the riders talk in the typewriter box at the bottom of the screen — speaker name + portrait chip; new calls queue up and play in order.
⛶ Fullscreen: F or the Fullscreen button — the stage (on phones, the whole handheld shell) fills the screen; Esc exits fullscreen.
