DIVEFALL — the formation hangs ahead. Pick a mode.
The dive: you are in the cockpit — the enemy formation hangs in the dark ahead of you. Ships peel off and dive straight at you along swooping curves. Shoot them before they reach you. DRIFTER (cyan) dives slow and straight. DART (magenta) dives fast and tight. WEAVER (gold) weaves side to side and takes two hits. SNATCHER (red) is the thief: if it reaches you while you still hold spare fighters, its capture beam steals one — shoot it down while it carries your fighter to win it back and unlock DUAL CANNONS.
Controls: MOUSE aim · CLICK / SPACE fire (hold) · B / SHIFT burst (3-shot fan) · V cockpit / chase view · P / START pause + save/load · M mute · R restart. On phones: drag the screen to aim, A fires, B bursts, D-pad nudges aim.
VR / AR: the ENTER VR and AR VIEW buttons run on the arcade vr-kit. With no headset available for testing they are labelled SIM — flat first-person is the verified mode.
First-person: the cockpit camera eases onto your aim and banks into the turn — it never snaps. The crosshair turns gold with a ◈ LOCK readout when it sits on a ship. The lower-left gauges read fighters, hostiles left and divers inbound at a glance; V swaps cockpit / chase view.
⛶ Fullscreen: F or the Fullscreen button — the stage (on phones, the whole handheld shell) fills the screen; Esc exits fullscreen.
