MAZE CHASE — You are PIP, a spark-runner loose in the neon maze. Eat all 408 sparks to clear the level; grab a surge orb and the hunters turn frightened — eat them for 200 · 400 · 800 · 1600. An ember fruit appears twice a level, worth 300–700. Four hunters run the documented targeting rules, each in their own style: BRAMBLE chases your tile, FABLE ambushes four tiles ahead of you, TIDE flanks off BRAMBLE's line, WISP hangs back when you get close. They scatter to their corners on a timer, then hunt again — faster every level.
Controls: arrows / WASD steer · V chase cam · P pause (save / load in the menu) · R restart · M mute.
WATCH is a personality bot playing PIP — ZEROX gambles on tight corners, TURTLE plays it safe, NEXUS splits the difference. It scores its options through the Fallible Engine and sometimes walks straight into a hunter. That is the show.
📱 Handheld shell: D-pad steers, A toggles chase cam, B pauses, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit).
First-person: V chase cam — eased follow camera with look-ahead + slew lean, hunter LOCK readout (gold + EAT! while frightened), PIP/sparks/surge gauges, speed lines on fast turns.
Fullscreen: F — the maze fills the screen (Esc exits first).
Clean-room: dot-chase formula, our own code, characters, maze, names and art — the four targeting rules are implemented from documented behaviour, nothing copied.
