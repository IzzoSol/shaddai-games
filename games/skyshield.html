SKYSHIELD — Warden Astra Vell holds the last lit coast. Enemy missiles rain from the dark; some split mid-flight into two or three warheads, bombers cross the sky dropping bombs, and smart bombs weave away from your blasts. Aim with the mouse / touch and click / SPACE to fire an interceptor from the nearest battery — its explosion catches anything inside, and a destroyed warhead bursts too, so chain your blasts. B fires at the nearest threat. V switches to battery-eye view: the sky fills the screen and the trails streak overhead at you — same rules, first-person. Wave clear pays a bonus for every city still standing plus leftover ammo; lose all six cities and the coast goes dark.
WATCH is a council bot defender on the Fallible Engine — a different council agent holds the batteries each wave, picking its shots (or holding fire) by personality, and learning from every wave. They misjudge on purpose — never frame-perfect. If the engine fails to load, the old SENTINEL / HOARDER / GAMBLER stand-ins defend instead.
📱 Handheld shell: on phones the game sits in its own console: D-pad moves the crosshair, A fires at it, B fires at the nearest threat, drag on the sky aims too, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit), SELECT toggles battery-eye view.
First-person: battery-eye view eases toward your aim, zooms on the crosshair, and shows a gold ◈ LOCK on the nearest threat in your sight — gauges read ammo, cities and range at a glance.
⛶ Fullscreen: F or the Fullscreen button — the stage (on phones, the whole handheld shell) fills the screen; Esc exits fullscreen.
Clean-room: Missile Command formula, our own code, cities, warden and art — no copied assets, names or code.
