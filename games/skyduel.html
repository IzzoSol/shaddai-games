SKYDUEL — two sky-hurlers stand on the rooftops of Cindral Reach: KESS the Stormcaller (emerald, hooded cloak and storm staff) against BRYN the Gale-Runner (gold, broad shoulders and a wind-torn scarf). Set your angle and power, then hurl a sunbolt across the city — gravity pulls it down, the wind pushes it sideways, and where it lands the rooftops crumble. Strike your rival dead-on to take the round; blast the roof out from under them and they fall. First to 3 rounds takes the match. The Watchmoon watches every throw.
↑ / ↓ angle · ← / → power · SPACE hurl · V rooftop-eye view · P pause · M mute · R restart. Drag on the skyline to aim by pointer, too.
WATCH is a council duel on the Fallible Engine — ZEROX gambles on huge power, TURTLE plays safe short lobs, NEXUS computes the arc; every choice is a recorded trace, and they miss on purpose, never frame-perfect. If the engine fails to load, the stand-in hurlers duel instead.
📱 Handheld shell: on phones the game sits in its own console: D-pad sets angle (up/down) and power (left/right), A hurls, B toggles rooftop-eye view, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit), SELECT mutes.
