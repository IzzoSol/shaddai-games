BLACKLINE — a GoldenEye-style objective FPS slice. VESPER, field operative, walks into the Halcyon blacksite with a suppressed pistol and three jobs: PHOTOGRAPH THE PROTOTYPE in the north-west vault, PLANT A TAP on the server rack in the north-east lab, and EXFILTRATE through the south-east vent. TROOPERS patrol the corridors with vision cones — get seen and the ALARM climbs, guards get faster and surer. SENTRY PRIME — 120 HP of armoured spite — walks the prototype approach. Finish all three objectives with the alarm at zero for the GHOST bonus.
W/S move · A/D strafe · ←/→ turn (or drag the view) · SHIFT run · SPACE / click fire · E interact / use · V automap · P / ESC pause · M mute · R restart.
WATCH is a council bot running the mission on the Fallible Engine — it pathfinds to each objective, aims at what it can see, and blunders on purpose. Never frame-perfect. DUEL is two alternate runs, one per player — high score takes the blacksite.
