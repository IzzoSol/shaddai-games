Four warlords. One fireball. Hold your gate.
CASTLEBREAK — Four warlords hold the corners of the Ember Court: VERA the Warden, KORG the Smith, MIRA the Tidecaller and SOLM the Judge. One fireball ricochets around the court and speeds up every time it is returned. Slide your shield along your gate to send it back — slip, and it smashes through your rune-stones and strikes your warlord. Last castle standing takes the court.
Controls: mouse / drag slides your shield along the gate, A / D or ← / → nudge it, SHIFT is a shield dash. In 2P, player 2 (Solm, gold) uses the arrow keys. V switches to behind-shield view — the camera drops to your gate and the fireball comes at you. P pauses (save / load in the menu).
First-person: in behind-shield view the camera eases toward the fireball as it closes on your gate — a gold ◈ LOCK marks the projected crossing, the shield console shows dash, gate position and ETA, and speed lines flare as the ball screams in.
WATCH is a 4-bot free-for-all with distinct styles — KORG chases every ball and overshoots, MIRA guards her core and never panics, SOLM gambles on wild lunges, VERA plays it steady. They misjudge on purpose — never frame-perfect.
⛶ Fullscreen: F or the Fullscreen button — the stage (on phones, the whole handheld shell) fills the screen; Esc exits fullscreen.
📱 Handheld shell: on phones the game sits in its own console: drag on the court (or D-pad) to slide your shield, A dashes, B toggles behind-shield view, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit), SELECT toggles the view.
