TUBESTORM — You are VEXA, a rim-rider guarding the storm-well. Climbers crawl up the lanes of the tube toward your rim — shoot them down the lane before they reach you. CRAWLERS climb straight, FLIPPERS hop lanes as they climb, SPIKERS leave spikes that eat your shots, PULSARS split into a fresh crawler when zapped. Clear a tube to ride the next shape — circle, square, triangle, star, hexagon — faster and meaner every time. STORM SURGE (B) is your panic button: it clears the whole tube, but charges are scarce.
Controls: ← → / A D slide the rim · SPACE / click fire · B / SHIFT Storm Surge · V first-person view · P pause (save / load in the menu) · R restart · M mute. Drag on the tube to slide the rider.
Fullscreen: F — the tube fills the screen (Esc exits first).
First-person: the camera eases with the rim — the gold LOCK bracket marks the climber in your lane, the THREAT bar shows the nearest climber's depth, and the pips show Storm Surge charges.
WATCH is a personality bot riding the rim — ZEROX chases every climber, TURTLE guards only the danger lanes, NEXUS splits the difference. It scores its options through the Fallible Engine and sometimes lets a flipper through. That is the show.
📱 Handheld shell: D-pad slides the rim, A fires, B Storm Surge, SELECT first-person, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit).
Clean-room: the tube-shooter formula (ride the rim, shoot down the lanes, climbers / flippers / spikes / superzapper, tube shapes that change per level) re-implemented from the documented behaviour of the 1981 tube shooter in our own code, with original characters, names, tube geometry and art — nothing copied.
