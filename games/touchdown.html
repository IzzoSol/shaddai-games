TOUCHDOWN — Pilot Juno Vale flies the lander KESTREL-9 down to the rust moon Emberfall. One physics integrator, generated terrain, and four lit pads: land soft on a pad and bank its multiplier — the NEEDLE ×3 pad is tight for a reason. ↑ / W / SPACE / A thrusts, ← → / A D rotates the craft, B fires the stabiliser (auto-levels, sips fuel). Watch the gauges: keep vertical speed under 2.0, horizontal under 1.4, and the legs level, or KESTREL-9 becomes a crater. Fuel is finite — a soft landing banks what is left. From level 3 the wind picks up and pushes you sideways.
WATCH is a bot pilot — NOVA flies steady and usually sticks it, GAMBLER burns late and sometimes lawn-darts, TURTLE creeps down safe and slow. Imperfect on purpose.
