TANKDUEL — Two steel pilots meet in the maze yard: NOVA (emerald, long lance barrel) and VEX (crimson, twin stub barrels). Shells ricochet off the walls — bank a shot around a corner and it still counts. First to 5 round wins takes the match (3 in Speed Trial).
Controls: W A S D drive · mouse aims the turret · SPACE / click fire. In 2P, player 2 (VEX) drives with the arrow keys and fires with ENTER — turrets lock forward, so you aim by turning your tank. V switches to periscope view — the camera drops into your cockpit. P pauses (save / load in the menu). Keys 1–6 pick a variation.
WATCH is a bot duel with distinct styles — NOVA holds her lane and aims true, VEX rushes in close and sprays, ORBIT banks angles off the walls, HALO waits for the perfect shot. They misjudge on purpose — never frame-perfect.
First-person: the periscope view now eases onto your turret with a gold ◈ LOCK readout (target · range · shell flight time), a bearing dial, hull pips and speed lines when you slew fast.
⛶ Fullscreen: F or the Fullscreen button — the stage (on phones, the whole handheld shell) fills the screen; Esc exits fullscreen.
📱 Handheld shell: twin virtual sticks — left stick drives, right stick aims the turret — FIRE shoots, B toggles periscope view, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit).
