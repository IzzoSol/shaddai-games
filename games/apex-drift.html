How to play: You are JUNO in the SOLARIS drift-rig, racing the elevated Skyworks — 3 laps against three council bots. The track floats above the city: go off the deck and you fall (damage + respawn at the last safe stretch). Hit the gold ramps fast to jump the drawbridge gaps — too slow and you drop. Hold drift through a corner to charge the boost meter, release for a surge. Cyan boost pads and floating SPARK CELLS (+100, instant boost) keep you fast. Scrape the rails and you take damage; at 100% you wreck and limp back at 35%.
Controls: ↑ / W throttle · ↓ / S brake · ← → / A D steer · SHIFT / SPACE drift · V hood cam · T switch track · P pause · M mute · R restart. Duel: P1 WASD + SHIFT, P2 arrows + /.
📱 Handheld shell: on phones the game sits in its own console: D-pad steers + throttles/brakes, A (hold) drifts, B toggles hood cam, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit), SELECT toggles hood cam.
WATCH is four council bots on the Fallible Engine — ZEROX gambles on every ramp and sometimes pays for it, TURTLE brakes for the gaps, NEXUS balances, ORACLE takes the inside line. They misjudge on purpose — never frame-perfect. Rubber-banding follows the documented Mario Kart 64 catch-up pattern (target speed offsets by position, SPEC lane — no code copied). If the engine fails to load, ROOK / SPARK / DRIFT stand-ins race instead.
