Aim the slinger, burst three of a kind.
ORBBURST — PIPPA the orb-slinger holds the grove against the falling orb-canopy. Aim with the mouse / drag and click / SPACE to fire — orbs bounce off the side walls and stick where they land. Match 3+ of a colour and they burst; anything left hanging with no path to the ceiling drops for bonus points. Every 6 shots the canopy pushes a new row down — let it cross the danger line and the grove is lost. Clear the whole board to take the level.
Versus: bursts of 4+ send slag rows onto your rival's board — slag never matches, it only falls when cut loose. First to force the other side to top out takes the round, first to 2 rounds takes the match.
WATCH is bot-vs-bot on the Fallible Engine — a different council agent slings each match, picking its angles by personality, and learning from every match. They misjudge on purpose — never frame-perfect. If the engine fails to load, the NOVA / TURTLE / GAMBLER stand-ins sling instead.
📱 Handheld shell: on phones the game sits in its own console: D-pad aims, A fires, B swaps the loaded orb, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit), SELECT toggles aim-cam.
First-person: aim-cam (V) zooms in behind PIPPA's shoulder so the board fills your view — same rules, first-person.
Clean-room: bubble-shooter formula, our own code, slinger, orbs and art — no copied assets, names or code. Reference: Frozen Bubble (complete GPL-2.0 open-source game, 100 levels + versus) was read about as a design reference only; nothing from it was run, copied or moved in.
