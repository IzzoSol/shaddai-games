🚀 Briefing: Pilot REN "EMBER" OKAFOR threads the KESTREL CORRIDOR in the interceptor VOIDWING — three sectors, two branch gates, one capital ship at the end: THE HOLLOW CROWN. Fly the gates to pick your route: the ION STORM (fast weavers, chain lightning) or the DERELICT RING (turret nests, heavy rock). Hold A / Space to charge a piercing shot, B / X to barrel-roll through fire, Shift / SELECT to boost.
🛩️ Wingmen: NEXUS and TURTLE fly your flanks and shoot what you don't — they can be shot down, and they re-form at the next sector. Their portraits are stacked-slice renders, same as every ship in the game: layered slices, rim light, engine glow — no flat blobs.
🎮 Controls: ←→↑↓ / W A S D steer · Space / Click fire (hold to charge) · X barrel roll · Shift boost · P pause.
📱 Handheld shell: D-pad steers, A fires (hold to charge), B barrel-rolls, SELECT boosts, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit).
