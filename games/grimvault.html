GRIMVAULT — a turn-based roguelike delve. Every step you take, the vault takes one too. Descend five floors, take the Core Shard from the CORE GUARDIAN's vault, and climb back to the surface stairs with it. Death is permanent — one life, no refunds.
ARROWS / WASD move (diagonals: QEZC) · SPACE / . wait a turn · V delver-cam (first-person) · P / ESC pause · M mute · R restart.
Bump a monster to attack it. Step on items to take them: gold, health cells, rations (food), torches (see further), scrolls (mapping reveals the floor, blast hurts everything you can see), power cells (+1 STR). Food drains every turn — starve and your HP drains instead. Kill things, earn XP, level up: +6 max HP, +1 STR.
ORIN, a rival delver, prowls floors 2–4 grabbing gold. Bump ORIN to fight for the hoard — slay ORIN and the hoard spills.
WATCH is a council bot delving on the Fallible Engine — it plans toward the stairs, the shard and home, flees when hurt, and blunders on purpose. Never frame-perfect. If the engine fails to load, the legacy VEX stand-in delves instead.
📱 Handheld shell: D-pad moves VEX-9, A waits a turn, B toggles delver-cam, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit).
