BUSINESS TYCOON (Stand Tycoon) — start small, scale big. Every morning: read the forecast, buy supplies at today's prices, set your price, open the doors and watch the street. Unsold perishables spoil overnight. Fair prices and a good recipe build reputation ★; gouging burns it. Profits buy upgrades and new properties across five districts on the city map — and the bank will lend against your net worth if you're short. Season: 30 days (45 for a data center). Score = net worth. Cash below zero at close = bankruptcy.
