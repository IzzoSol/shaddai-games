HIGH ROLLER — six-max No-Limit Texas Hold'em against the council. Blinds 10/20, everyone starts with 1,000 chips, busted seats rebuy 1,000 (the chip supply is tracked, so every chip on the table is accounted for — chips are never created or destroyed mid-hand). Full betting rounds with min-raise tracking and proper side pots: an all-in player can only win the pots they paid into. Win by taking the pot — by showdown or by making five very expensive robots fold.
The bots play real poker math — Chen-formula preflop strength, made-hand and draw equity, pot odds — scored through the Fallible Engine, so their personalities leak: ZEROX bluffs and shoves, TURTLE folds anything marginal, ORACLE slowplays monsters and traps, PIKADON defends with ice in its veins. WATCH seats six bots and lets the engine run the table — every decision is traced, every hand recorded, and the cast rotates hand by hand.
