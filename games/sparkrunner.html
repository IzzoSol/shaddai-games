SPARKRUNNER — courier-bot JOLT sprints the neon jungle of Ember Hollow, gathering lost Sparks before the clock runs out. Every scene is grown from one reversible LFSR: 255 possible jungle screens, each with its own pits, thorn logs, spark-eels, vines, climb-walls and treasure — the same seed always grows the same jungle. ← → / A D run, SPACE / W / ↑ jump, B / X grab a vine and swing, ↑ ↓ climb a wall ladder. Fall in a pit, touch a thorn log or a spark-eel and you lose a life; quicksand slows you down. Reach the far edge to enter the next scene — finish all 12 scenes before 3:00 and the leftover time pays a bonus. V switches to chase cam: the camera rides right behind JOLT. M mutes, P pauses, R restarts.
WATCH is two council bots racing the same jungle on the Fallible Engine — a rotating pair, each with its own style: some gamble on late jumps and grab every spark, others jump early and play it safe. They mistime jumps on purpose and sometimes lawn-dart into the pits. RACE puts you against QUILL's ghost on the same seed.
📱 Handheld shell: on phones the game sits in its own console: D-pad runs and climbs, A jumps, B swings, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit), SELECT toggles chase cam.
Clean-room: the jungle-runner formula (LFSR-grown scenes, run / jump / climb / swing) re-implemented from the documented behaviour of the classic 1982 jungle runner in our own code — SPEC LANE, read as text/docs only. All names, characters, scenes, art and tuning are original; no copied code, assets or names.
