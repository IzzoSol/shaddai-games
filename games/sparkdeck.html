SPARKDECK — a pocket card battler, built clean-room from the documented mechanics of the pocket card-battle formula (SPEC lane: deck, energy, bench + active, prizes on knockouts; every creature, name, type and line of text here is original). Twelve original Sparks across four types — EMBER beats GALE, GALE beats STONE, STONE beats TIDE, TIDE beats EMBER (double damage, half on the losing side). Each turn: draw one card, attach one energy, play Sparks to your bench, then attack or pass. Knock a rival Spark out and take a prize card — first to 3 prizes wins; knock out the last Spark on the table and the match is yours.
Click a hand card to play it (creature → bench, energy → active) · ← → pick a hand card · Enter / A play it · 1 / 2 attack · E attach energy · R retreat · N / B end turn · V table-cam · P pause · M mute.
WATCH is two council bots duelling on the Fallible Engine — each picks its next play from scored options by personality, so it overcommits energy, attacks into a bad type matchup, and tilts after a lost prize. Never frame-perfect. If the engine fails to load, local stand-ins play instead.
📱 Handheld shell: on phones the game sits in its own console: D-pad picks a hand card, A plays it, B ends the turn, » also ends the turn, CAM zooms the table, START opens the menu (resume / save state / load state / restart / quit).
